Single-player prototype for a telekinetic Terraria character. The fantasy is remotely
operating ordinary physical objects, not replacing combat with generic magic attacks.

Prototype scope:
- Toggleable telekinetic remote control (default G) with a compact HUD status strip
- Remote grip has finite travel speed and must physically reach the cursor
- Remote ordinary swinging/thrusting melee with grip-derived swing collision
- Remote shortswords, spears, and lances
- Projectile-emitting melee (Ice Blade, Enchanted Sword) fires from the grip and auto-aims
  at the nearest visible enemy
- Remote pickaxes, axes, and hammers; tile effects are independent from melee collision
- Remote tile/wall placement
- Remote operation based on connected open space inside the visible screen area
- Cursor-steered thrown consumable explosives while the throwing click is held
- Hold-to-levitate self-flight (default Left Shift), becoming a toggle once duration is infinite
- Hold-to-grab enemy manipulation (default V), sharing one charge pool with levitation
- Duration is the only progression variable; it scales with permanent max HP and recharges
  while sustained telekinesis is not in use
- Large fixed vanilla pickup range (60 tiles), with no pickup pathfinding

Intentionally not included:
- Yoyos, boomerangs, flails
- Drills and chainsaws
- Terragrim and other unusual special swords
- Launcher ammunition as steerable explosives
- Multiplayer synchronization
